Why Cold Brew?

Cold brew is made by steeping coarse coffee grounds in cold water for an extended period (12-24 hours). Because heat is not used, the chemical compounds that cause bitterness and acidity are not fully extracted.

This results in a cup that is incredibly smooth, naturally sweet, and up to 60% less acidic than hot-brewed coffee. Our calculator helps you nail the perfect coffee-to-water ratio.

  • Use Extra-Coarse Grind: Your coffee should look like raw sugar or breadcrumbs. Fine grinds will over-extract and make the brew muddy.
  • Ratio is Everything: A 1:8 ratio creates a strong concentrate (to be diluted with ice/milk), while 1:12 to 1:15 is perfect for ready-to-drink coffee.

The 5 Golden Rules of Cold Brew

  1. Filtered Water Only: Since coffee is 98% water, and cold brew steeps for so long, using clean, filtered water is essential.
  2. Room Temp vs Fridge: Steeping at room temperature extracts faster (12-16 hours). Steeping in the fridge takes longer (18-24 hours) but yields a slightly cleaner flavor.
  3. Filter Well: Use a paper filter or cheesecloth after steeping to remove all fine particles. Silt at the bottom of your cup will turn bitter over time.

Troubleshooting Your Brew

My cold brew is too bitter or harsh

You likely steeped it for too long, or your grind was too fine. Try reducing the steep time by 2-4 hours, or coarsen your grind setting.

My cold brew is sour or weak

This is under-extraction. Either your grind was too coarse (like pebbles), or you didn't steep it long enough. Try letting it sit for another 4-6 hours.

How long does it last?

Once filtered, cold brew concentrate can be stored in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks without losing its flavor.

Enjoy the smooth, refreshing taste of your homemade cold brew!

Refrigeration safety reference: the U.S. FDA recommends keeping refrigerators at or below 4°C, using covered containers, and consuming prepared food promptly. See the FDA food-storage guidance。
