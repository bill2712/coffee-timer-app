    What is the AeroPress?

    The AeroPress is a revolutionary coffee maker that combines immersion and pressure. It's incredibly versatile, allowing you to brew anything from a thick, espresso-like concentrate to a clean, filter-style cup.

    Because of the pressure generated by pressing the plunger, extraction happens very quickly. This calculator focuses on the popular 'Inverted Method', which gives you full control over immersion time.

    AeroPress Pro Tips

    1. Use the Inverted Method: Assemble the plunger and chamber upside down. This prevents water from dripping through the filter before you want it to.
    2. Rinse the paper filter: Always rinse the filter in the cap to remove papery taste and help it stick to the plastic.
    3. Press gently: Do not force the plunger. A gentle, steady press over 30 seconds yields a sweeter, cleaner cup.

    Common Mistakes

    1. Plunging too fast

    If you press down with too much force, you'll compress the coffee bed and channel the water, resulting in bitter flavors. Let the weight of your arms do the work.

    2. Using boiling water

    The AeroPress relies on pressure to aid extraction, so you don't need boiling water. Try temperatures around 80-85°C for dark roasts, and 90-93°C for light roasts.

    3. Grinding too coarse

    Since the contact time is short, a medium-fine grind (similar to table salt) works best. Too coarse, and your coffee will be sour and weak.
