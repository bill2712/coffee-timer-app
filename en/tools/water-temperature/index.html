Why Predict Water Temperature?

Water temperature is critical for extraction. Boiling water can cause over-extraction and bitterness.

Using a thermometer every time disrupts workflow.

This tool uses physics to calculate the exact wait time for your water to naturally cool to the perfect target temperature.

Important limitation: this is an estimate based on preset cooling coefficients. Water volume, kettle geometry, lid position, airflow, and the kettle’s initial temperature all affect cooling; use a food thermometer to confirm the actual temperature.
