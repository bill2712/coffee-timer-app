A purchase order that avoids waste

  1. 1Choose the method you will really use each week.
  2. 2A normal kettle can get you started; upgrade to a gooseneck only when pouring becomes the V60 bottleneck.
  3. 3A scale improves repeatability; a burr grinder usually affects consistency more directly than decorative accessories.
  4. 4After 5–10 brews, use your records to decide whether temperature control, a server, or extra filters solve a real problem.
