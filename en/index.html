The Science of the 3-Stage Pour

How to balance acidity, sweetness, and body through precise timing and water flow.

1. Blooming

Pour 2-2.5x the coffee weight in water to wet the grounds, wait ~40 seconds. This releases trapped CO2 (degassing), expanding the coffee bed and creating the perfect foundation for even extraction.

2. Main Extraction (Sweetness)

The first major pour makes up 60% of total water. High temperature and steady circular pouring extract the bright fruit acids and sweet compounds. This stage determines the primary flavor balance.

3. Final Control (Strength)

The final 40% adjusts the concentration. As water cools and most solubles are already extracted, switch to a gentle center pour or very soft circles to prevent over-extraction and woody bitterness.
